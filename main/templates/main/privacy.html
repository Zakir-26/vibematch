Privacy Policy

Last Updated 24th Sep 2026

Welcome to VibeMatch. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use the VibeMatch website.

By using VibeMatch, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

When you create an account or use VibeMatch, we may collect information such as:

  • Email address
  • Password
  • Name
  • Age
  • Gender
  • Location
  • Profile picture
  • Interests and preferences
  • Relationship preferences
  • Social media information that you choose to provide

Or any other additional information you provided

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your VibeMatch account
  • Help users discover and connect with other users
  • Provide personalized matching and recommendation features
  • Help you to find your perfect match

Profile Information

VibeMatch is designed to help users discover and connect with other people.

Information that you choose to include in your public profile, such as your username, name, age, profile picture, interests, and other profile information, may be visible to other VibeMatch users.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in your public profile that you do not want other users to see.

Email Address

We may use your email address to:

  • Verify your email address
  • Help you recover your account
  • Send important account-related notifications, Like when someone Winks 😉 to you
  • Respond to your support requests

We do not intend to sell your email address to third parties, nor we will annoy you with our marketing emails until you allow us.

Passwords

Your password is used to authenticate your account. VibeMatch does not intentionally store passwords in plain text. Passwords are handled through the authentication system used by the website.

You should choose a strong, unique password and never share it with another person.

Social Login

If you choose to sign in using a third-party authentication provider, such as a social login service, that provider may share certain account information with VibeMatch according to your authorization and that provider's privacy policy.

We only use information provided through social login for purposes related to creating and managing your VibeMatch account.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

VibeMatch may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you signed in
  • Maintain your session
  • Remember certain preferences
  • Help the website function correctly

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may prevent some features of VibeMatch from working correctly.

User-to-User Communication

If VibeMatch provides features such as messaging, chat, or other communication tools, information you voluntarily share through those features may be accessible to the users you communicate with.

Please exercise caution when sharing personal information with other users.

Never share passwords, financial information, verification codes, or other highly sensitive information with another user.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to:

  • Access information associated with your account
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Update your profile
  • Delete your account
  • Request deletion of certain personal information
  • Withdraw certain permissions or consent

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

Account Deletion

If you no longer want to use VibeMatch, you may request deletion of your account and associated personal information.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or legitimate business purposes.

Children's Privacy

VibeMatch is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older.

We do not knowingly allow individuals under 18 to create or maintain accounts on VibeMatch.

If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 18, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

Third-Party Websites

VibeMatch may contain links to third-party websites or services, including social media platforms.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing them with personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as VibeMatch develops or our practices change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
